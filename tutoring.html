About Tutoring

Tutoring

I enjoy tutoring and would like to do more of it. My specialties are writing (including college essays) and the humanities.

In college, I received the Le Baron Briggs Prize for Fiction. I also graduated with Highest Honors and was awarded the Taliesin Prize for the breadth and rigor of my coursework.

I have 5+ years of experience as a tutor and was an instructor at the Harvard College Writing Center.

Additionally, as a former attendee of Iowa Young Writers' Studio and a multiple-time Scholastic national medalist, I'm familiar with the high school competition writing scene.

I help students work towards their immediate goals while teaching skills and frameworks that can scaffold a lifetime of writing.
