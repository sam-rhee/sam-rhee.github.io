About Tutoring

Tutoring

I enjoy tutoring and would like to do more of it. My specialties are writing (including college essays) and the humanities.

In particular, as a former attendee of Iowa Young Writers' Studio and a multiple-time Scholastic national medalist, I'm familiar with the high school competition writing scene.

I focus not only on helping my students succeed at their immediate goals, but also on teaching them skills, texts, and frameworks that will scaffold their writing lives long-term.

I have 5+ years of experience as a tutor and was an instructor at the Harvard College Writing Center.
